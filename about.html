About Coderra

We started Coderra because good software shouldn't take six months.

A small, senior team that designs, builds, and ships — without the agency overhead.

Our story

Built by people who were tired of slow builds.

Coderra was founded by a small group of engineers and designers who kept seeing the same problem: startups needed software fast, but agencies moved slow and freelancers disappeared halfway through.

So we built a studio around fixed scopes, fixed prices, and a small senior team on every project — no juniors learning on your budget, no scope creep, no disappearing acts.
